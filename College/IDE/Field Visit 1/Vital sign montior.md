# Things that it shows:

- **Heart Rate (HR) or Pulse Rate (PR):** Shown as a number in beats per minute (bpm), usually at the top of the screen.

- **Electrocardiogram (ECG):** Displayed as a continuous moving wave line that tracks the electrical activity and rhythm of the heart.

- **Blood Pressure (BP / NIBP):** Shown as a fraction representing systolic (peak pressure) and diastolic (resting pressure) measurements.

- **Oxygen Saturation (SpO₂):** Shown as a percentage indicating the amount of oxygen carried in the blood, measured via a small finger clip.

- **Respiratory Rate (RR):** Shown as the number of breaths a patient takes per minute, often accompanied by a breathing wave line.

- **Body Temperature:** Displayed in Celsius or Fahrenheit.
