LLMs are basically large neural networks that are trained to behave like humans on certain tasks.
- examples include: GPT-5, BERT, DeepSeek.

- LLMs are able to understand the context and relation between texts
- Their architecture is majorly based on transformer model that includes a self attention mechanism to get relationship between words

> Mostly LLMs are used as only chatbots, until recently when they are being used as agents to develop workflows and automations.

- LLMs take input as [[Embeddings]] vectors which it can then process to generate an output vector (the next word).
